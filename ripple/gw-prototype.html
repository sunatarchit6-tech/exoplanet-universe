You are watching
spacetime ripple.

The first gravitational wave humans ever directly detected.

Two black holes — 35.6 and 30.6 times the mass of the Sun — collided 1.3 billion light-years away. The wave they made took 1.3 billion years to reach Earth, where LIGO measured it on September 14, 2015.

What you see here is the same event, in three dimensions, slowed down and visualised so you can explore it from any angle.
