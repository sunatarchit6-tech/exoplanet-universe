does the universe groove · audio-only test
The only question this test answers: when you layer three chirps from different binary masses on a slow loop, does it sound like something you'd send to a friend, or does it sound like a fax machine?

Three tracks. Each is a real gravitational-wave chirp synthesised from masses you set. Track A is heavy and bass-y (think GW190521 territory). Track B is canonical (GW150914). Track C is light and high (small BBH, almost BNS). Toggle the boxes to set rhythms. Press play.

If after a few minutes of fiddling this sounds actually pleasant — even genuinely musical — we have a product. If it sounds like rising sweeps stacking with no groove, we don't, and we ship Ripple instead.
